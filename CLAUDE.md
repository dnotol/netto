# CLAUDE.md

## Project

Netto tells a Belgian SRL owner-director how to pay themselves for the year so that the most money reaches their own pocket: the split between director pay and dividends, costs and benefits in kind, advance tax payments. It runs locally only; there is no production and no hosting yet.

**Docs map.** Each has one job.

| Where | Holds |
|---|---|
| `CLAUDE.md` (this file) | Instructions only: what an agent must do, must not do, or would otherwise get wrong. No status, no history, no domain spec. Edit it when a slice changed a convention, an architecture rule or a gotcha. |
| Notion, « Netto Development » | Every unit of work (Work Items, User Stories), the product contract (the « Goal & scope » page: what v1 is and is not) and the Lever catalogue (one page per fiscal lever, with its official sources). There is no backlog file, and what shipped is the git history. |

| The subsystem maps, an Artifact each: the company car ([link](https://claude.ai/artifact/AhoWfP6DmKmqWatT2xAc1j)), the copyright royalty ([link](https://claude.ai/artifact/H78okHrfkQ1Fjs3FtCYGCY)) | One mechanism that reaches beyond one card or one layer, in depth: what it hands the year and where each of its figures goes, diagrams of how it computes, what the engine tests across layers, its inputs and refusals, its amounts with the `official-docs/` files they come from, a worked example from the tests, where it lives in the code, its dated decisions and what is not built yet. |

The code wins where the two differ. |

**Always current: the River Map, the subsystem maps and the official documents.**

- **A map is updated in the slice that changes what it draws** (a module, a bucket, a rule, a figure it quotes), in place at the same URL: read it first (`Artifact read`, then `path: "index.html"` for its file, whose publish skeleton is stripped before republishing), add a dated rule, and bump its date and the `main` commit in its footer. A slice that changes a mechanism with a map of its own updates that map, and the River Map only where the river itself changed. The hivemind does it, since drones cannot publish an Artifact: a drone's report says what its slice changed on a map, and the hivemind updates the map when it integrates that slice.
- **Every complex part of the domain has a clear diagram, as current as the code**: the whole river on the River Map, and each mechanism that reaches beyond one card or one layer (a company car: its costs off the profit, its benefit in kind taxed as pay, what Corporate tax adds back and tests, the car weighed in cash against the same car bought privately) on a subsystem map of its own, which the River Map links. That mechanism's rules, amounts and decisions live on its map, not on the River Map or in this file. A map is drawn while the mechanism is analysed, before its first slice, marks what is not decided yet as such, and is redrawn in the slice that changes the mechanism, never left showing what the code no longer runs.
- **`official-docs/` is checked for newer editions regularly**: when a story's slices are analysed, for the documents that the rule sets of each layer they touch cite (a newer coordinated article on Fisconetplus, a new indexation notice, a law or programme law published since; a forager per layer), and each year once the next income year's amounts are published. A newer edition is filed (downloading needs the operator's go) and the old one marked superseded, and the rule set that cites it is checked against it in the same story. Unattended, the hivemind works from the forager's reading, logs it, and files the document at landing once the operator gives the go.
- **Every Artifact that states Netto's rules or figures is faithful to `official-docs/`**, the River Map and the subsystem maps first: each rule and amount it shows is the one the current documents give for its income year, and it changes when they do.

## Where work comes from

**Work is operator-picked and never self-started, and whatever is picked runs through the hivemind.**

The tracker is Notion: the « Netto Development » page, holding **Work Items** (the executable unit, typed Task / Bug / Technical, filed under an `Area`: Tax engine · Web app · Content · Plumbing · Intendance), **User Stories** (a user-facing outcome grouping several items; a technical item with no story is normal) and the **Lever catalogue**. `Status` runs Backlog → Selected → Doing → Done, and **`Selected` is the operator's pick**. A lever is in scope only once the operator sets it to `In v1`.

- **Read Notion freely**, starting with « Goal & scope » before planning anything.
- **Write narrowly, and only the hivemind writes.** Drones report what should change. While implementing: set `Doing` / `Done` on the items being worked, create a new item at `Backlog` for something discovered mid-slice, and edit « Goal & scope » where a slice changed the contract. When the task is plainly analysis, writing the related pages is the work. Never re-prioritise, re-file or reorganise unless asked.
- **A hive outlives its session.** Its state is in Notion and git: the items' statuses, the slice and story branches, and the decision log on the story. `/skillz:hivemind resume <story>` picks it up. A run that ends unfinished says what is done and what is left in a comment on the story.

## Methodology

- **Every picked story or item runs through `/skillz:hivemind`**: one session on Fable leads it, a drone builds each slice on a branch of its own, and the hivemind integrates the slices onto one story branch, `feature/<story-slug>`, which carries their focused commits and is fast-forwarded onto `main` when the story is done (`git-branching`). A size-S request the hivemind builds itself, on one branch.
- **Attended or unattended.** Unattended, a run starts with a kickoff while the operator is there, then decides what it can, logs every decision on the story under « Decisions taken without you », and parks only critical questions. Nothing reaches `main` without the operator.
- **Work is named, never numbered**: a Work Item is cited by its title.
- The plan gate is the approval: no drone builds before the operator approved the plan, at the kickoff when unattended. Minimal diffs: no refactoring outside the current task.

## Parallel work

What the hivemind needs to run a hive here.

- **Start the hivemind from the main checkout**, not from a worktree session. Its story branch and its drones live in worktrees under `.claude/worktrees/`, and Rider keeps the main checkout.
- **Drones load** `csharp-standards` and `csharp-testing`.
- **Build and test** from the worktree's root, in Release: `dotnet build -c Release`, `dotnet test -c Release`, and the fast loop `dotnet test tests/Netto.Tests.Unit -c Release`. No test needs Docker.
- **The app from a worktree.** Rider's AppHost holds ports 5274 and 15187, so serve `Netto.Web` alone: `dotnet run --project src/Netto.Web -c Release --launch-profile http -- --urls http://localhost:5290`, from the worktree, in the background. Stop it before the next build of that worktree, whose `Netto.Web.dll` it locks. `--no-launch-profile` runs in Production, where the WebAssembly assets are not served. Port 5290 is the hivemind's: it runs the browser checks, drones verify with tests.
- **Hot files** that slices tend to share: `src/Netto.Core/Simulation/YearSimulation.cs`, `src/Netto.Web.Client/River/RiverInputs.cs` with `PlanStorage.cs` and `PlanComposition.cs` beside it, `BucketsView.cs`, `src/Netto.Web/wwwroot/app.css` with `Layout/NettoTheme.cs`, `src/Netto.Web.Client/Pages/Plan.razor` with `Tabs/PlanTab.cs`, `Tabs/PlanTabBar.razor` and `Tabs/TabBadge.cs`, and `Directory.Packages.props`. Two slices that both write one of them are built one after the other.
- **Exclusive resources beyond the defaults:**
  - Raising `PlanStorage.Version`: one slice of a story raises it, and a later slice of the same story adds to that format (« Architecture »).
  - The maps and the design system: only the hivemind publishes an Artifact.
  - `official-docs/`: only the hivemind files a document, after the operator's go to download it.
  - Tax-Calc: only the hivemind drives it, and each submission needs the operator's go. Unattended, a test's expected value is derived from the official amounts, the test says so, and the Tax-Calc check is left for landing.
  - « Goal & scope » and this file: a drone reports what its slice changed, and the hivemind edits them.

## Architecture

- **Projects and dependency direction**: `Netto.Web.Client` → `Netto.Core`; `Netto.Web` → `Netto.Web.Client`; `Netto.AppHost` → `Netto.Web`; tests → `Netto.Core`, `Netto.Web.Client`. Never the reverse.
- **`Netto.Core` is the tax engine and stays a plain library**: no ASP.NET Core dependency (`CoreDependencyTests` fails if one appears) and nothing the browser runtime cannot run (`SupportedPlatform=browser` raises CA1416; fix it, never suppress it). That is what lets the engine move behind an API later without a rewrite.
- **Every page is WebAssembly and none is prerendered.** `App.razor` sets the render mode once, on `<Routes>` and `<HeadOutlet>`, so the server never runs a component and a user's figures only ever exist in the browser. Pages live in `Netto.Web.Client` and carry **no** `@rendermode` line; declaring one inside the already-interactive tree throws. Never add prerendering, a server-side component or a server endpoint that receives user figures without the operator deciding it.
- **`Netto.Web` only serves the app.** If Netto ever gets an API, it goes there, not in a new project.
- **No tax rule outside `Netto.Core`.** A component growing an `if` about tax behaviour means that logic belongs in Core. Core returns data and error keys, never display text; the page turns a key into English.
- **The engine follows the law's order of computation** (Notion, « Engine architecture »): layer calculators composed by one year simulation (`YearSimulation.Run(Scenario)`), and each module card of the river reads its steps, tagged by layer and taxable event, instead of calculating on its own; a new income year is a rule set, a lever is scenario inputs plus a rule in the layer it touches plus its steps. What the company pays the director besides pay (the vouchers, the expense allowances, the home office rent) comes off `Scenario.ProfitBeforeDirector`, revenue less running costs, before the split shares the rest (`YearSimulation.ProfitBeforePay`); what the company cannot deduct of it is added back to its taxable income (`YearResult.DisallowedExpenses`), and a lever's steps join the ledger only when it is used, so a year without it keeps its steps. The plausible swap seams are the regional tax and how director pay is paid. A search across pays (`SplitSearch`) runs `YearSimulation.Level`, the year's figures without its steps or the next 1,000, never arithmetic of its own, with every other input held, the advance payments among them; `SplitSearchTests` holds each pay it draws to `Run`. The moves Netto recommends from the plan they are measured from (Today, or while no plan is starred, the plan in the river as it was opened or saved: « The Optimisation tab » below) are found the same way (`MoveSearch`): each kind of move has a finder (`IMoveFinder`: the split's best pay; the company's and the director's advance payments that cancel each surcharge by the first deadline not yet past on the date the search is given, from the browser's clock through `TimeProvider`; the eco and sport/culture vouchers at their maxima; the home office rent at the cap once its facts are given; letting the copyright royalty follow the pay once a ruling accepts some at it and that plan's royalty is fixed below that (`CopyrightFollowsPayFinder`; the move holds a pay above what the company pays beside the royalty, on the royalty's map), after which the split's own move weighs pay and royalty together; never the flat expense allowances, worth nothing to a director without an advance ruling Netto cannot see) proposing its move from where the plan stands, the move that adds the most is taken and the others asked again on top of it, until none adds a euro, and a move the engine refuses or that leaves less than the reserve is never taken; every gain is the difference of two `YearSimulation.Run`s, so the moves' gains sum to the plan's pocket less the measured-from plan's. A move of a kind already taken (`Move.Kind`: its value, a payment's deadline) replaces it in its line, which adds both gains (`Move.After`: a payment adds to the earlier one), so the Optimisation tab shows one line per value. Each line carries the years before its first round and after its last (`PlannedMove.Before`, `After`), taken from runs the search already makes, so the tab says why a move helps without running the year again. A new kind of move is a `Move` record, its finder, its words on the Optimisation tab (`OptimisationTabText`), why it helps (`Tabs/MoveReasons`) and the card it links to (`MoveLinks`).
- **A thing the company puts at the director's disposal is a module of its own behind one plain contract** (Notion, « Engine architecture »). The company car lives in `Netto.Core/CompanyCar/` (`CarFacts`, `CarRules` per income year, `CarCalculator`), tested on its own, and its card, view models and texts in `Netto.Web.Client/River/CompanyCar/`; how it computes, and where each of its figures goes, is on its map. It hands the year one result, `BenefitInKind` (`Netto.Core/BenefitsInKind/`), plain figures a laptop or a phone will give the same way. `YearSimulation` composes it beside the other levers and is the only engine code that names the car: the costs less the contribution come off `ProfitBeforePay`, the costs a cost of the company like its running costs (operator, 2026-10-23), in the ledger as a step on `Track.TaxBaseOnly` that the bar of buckets leaves out of the river's start (`BucketsView.StartOf`), and the contribution leaves the pocket for the company; the benefit goes into `GrossToNet` as `benefitInKind`, taxable pay never paid in cash (in the contributions, the tax, the key formula's withholding and the assessment; never in the net in pocket or the company's cost of the pay), and into `CorporateTaxCalculator` as `flatRateBenefits`; `NotDeductible` joins `YearResult.DisallowedExpenses`. Nothing car-specific in DirectorPay, PersonalTax, SocialContributions or CorporateTax: the boundary is a convention checked at review, not a test. A step that rests on more texts than its own names them in `Step.AlsoCites`; the amounts a step cites are the ones the calculator used, handed back with its result (`CarResult.Co2Amounts`, `DeductionAmounts`), and a rule set's dates (a deduction period's start, a fake hybrid's first year) are plain `DateOnly` values beside its amounts, as an age band's month is.
- **A copyright royalty is a module of its own behind one plain contract.** `Netto.Core/Copyright/` holds its facts (`CopyrightRoyalty`), `CopyrightRules` per income year (2026, and 2025 for the Tax-Calc tests) and `CopyrightCalculator`; its card, view models and texts live in `Netto.Web.Client/River/Copyright/`, the card after the split at the head of the director's stream, while the split decides its mode and fixed amount (`CopyrightInputs.FollowsPay`, `SplitRoyaltyView`); how its limits split it and how each part is taxed is on its map. A rule that changes from one year to the next, such as who takes the flat-rate costs (`CopyrightFlatRateCosts.NeedsAttestation`), is the year's rule data, never a test on the year. It hands the year `MovableIncomePaid` (`Netto.Core/MovableIncome/`): the amount, what is paid apart (`PaidApart`, which reaches the pocket), the part taxed apart (`IncomeTaxedApart`, its net with its rate), the part taxed as pay and what was withheld. `YearSimulation` is the only engine code that names it: the amount comes off `ProfitBeforePay`, deducted in full; `GrossToNet` takes the movable income: the part taxed as pay is pay paid in cash besides the payslip (`RequalifiedPay`: in `TaxablePay`, the contributions, the scale, the art. 157 base and the net; never in `CompanyCost`, the statement or the key formula), and art. 215 reads `TaxablePayWithoutRequalified`; the part taxed apart is on the year's figures only, `TaxedApartTax` what it adds on top of the pay and the rent. `PersonalTaxCalculator` takes an `IncomeTaxedApart`, and the assessment credits what was withheld (`Assessment.WithheldBesidesPay`). Nothing copyright-specific in DirectorPay, PersonalTax, SocialContributions or CorporateTax. A royalty is a fixed amount or follows the pay (`CopyrightRoyalty.FollowsPay`): then at each pay it is what an advance ruling accepts there (`CopyrightCalculator.Ceiling`), which `Run` and `Level` resolve from the scenario's own pay before anything reads it, `YearResult.Scenario` holding the amount it came to; with no creative share, the four previous years above the cap or a year without an envelope rate it is no royalty, never a refusal, and `CopyrightResult.Following` says why, so a card applies no rule of its own. The split's largest pay is `YearSimulation.PayCeiling`, the largest pay the company pays together with its royalty, and the pay keeping the reduced rate is found by halving, since the royalty lowers the taxable income as the pay rises. A fixed royalty above what the company has before pay and royalty is refused (`copyright_royalty_above_profit`); a following one never is.
- **Rules come only from official, current sources** (tax administration, Moniteur belge, INASTI), each amount with its source and income year. An amount that is not published yet is marked as such, never guessed; a rule set that borrows the latest published amounts records the year they were set for (`MunicipalRates.RatesIncomeYear`). The Lever catalogue holds the research; the document an amount is read from is filed in `official-docs/` (downloading one needs the operator's go), and a rule set cites only documents current for its income year. Each `Source` names that file in `Document`, the path `INDEX.md`'s *File* column gives, and `SourceDocumentTests` fails when an amount of a rule set, or a source its steps cite, points to a document `INDEX.md` does not list as `current` for that income year. A static step source (`PersonalTaxRules.IncomeTaxScale`) is held to every year the steps run for, so it has to become per year once the next year's rule sets exist; a year that borrows a document is in its *Applies to* (`official-docs/README.md`). A CIR 92 amount cites the administration's coordinated article for that income year on Fisconetplus (« Article 130, CIR 92 (revenus 2026) »), which carries the year's indexed amounts; not an SPF summary page, which can lag a reform (the rates page still showed the pre-reform 2026 tax-free amount in October 2026), nor Justel's CIR 92, frozen since 2002. A card's list of conditions (`VoucherRules.Conditions` and the like) is held to the same documents: a condition whose text is not filed waits for it rather than showing unsourced.
- **One rules record per layer, one instance per income year** (`SocialContributionRules.IncomeYear2026`, `PersonalTaxRules.IncomeYear2025`, found through `For(year)`). Every amount is a `RuleAmount` carrying its `Source`, and rates are fractions (20.50% is `0.205m`); `RuleAmountListTests` holds every `RuleAmount` of the pay's, the taxes' and the withholding's rule sets to their hand-kept `*Amounts` lists. A new year is a new instance, never an edit of last year's.
- **The director's monthly withholding is its own rule set** (`WithholdingRules.IncomeYear2026`, the key formula's steps in `WithholdingCalculator`, each line rounded as the SPF simulator's formula chain rounds it). Its amounts cite annex III as decreed on 11.12.2025 (« Règles 1er janvier 2026 ») and its method the key formula: the coordinated annex III on Fisconetplus shows the latest amendment's value of n° 32 (3,089.63 from 1 November 2026), not January's. The rule set dates the rules it holds (`RulesFrom`) and the ones it does not model yet (`UnmodelledRulesFrom`), and the statement flags the months from that date. The pay statement (`GrossToNetResult.Statement`) and the balance on the assessment (`GrossToNetResult.Assessment`, by `AssessmentCalculator`) are records beside the ledger, not steps. Another income taxed with the pay, the home office's net rent, goes into `GrossToNet` as `otherIncome`: the pay's own figures (its steps, its net, its statement) stay the pay alone's, as art. 158 takes the pay for the surcharge; `GrossToNetResult.OtherIncomeTax` is what the rent adds (the tax on the total less the pay's, the credit for low earned income it takes away, the surcharge that lost credit adds), and `AdvancePayments`, `Assessment` and `NextThousand` are on the whole income (Tax-Calc T13 and T14). `TaxPrepayments.Withheld` is null for the key formula's withholding, which `GrossToNet` derives from the pay before the surcharge; `IncomeTaxAdvancePaymentCalculator` refuses a null.
- **Round where the administration rounds**: `Money.RoundToCent` (half away from zero) on each line its calculation detail shows rounded, not once at the end. INASTI bills four rounded quarters; Tax-Calc rounds the flat-rate costs, the basic tax, the federal share, the Walloon tax and the tax credit for low earned income each on its own. Caps, bounds and the searches by halving round through `Money.DownToCent` and `UpToCent`; the smallest advance payment that cancels a surcharge is `Money.SmallestCovering`, the smallest amount in cents whose rounded credit covers what is left, for the company's and the director's calculators alike (the company's top-ups follow the director's reading since 2026-10-24, by cents).
- **Eligibility is declared by the user, never decided by the engine**: Core takes what it cannot see as a status (VVPRbis shares and their distribution, a small company the reduced corporate rate is open to, its first four tax periods and its first three financial years, both set in the river by one choice of the company's financial year, the director's first three years of a first self-employed activity, who pays the director's social contributions, `ContributionsPaidBy`, the days worked that the meal vouchers match, the share of the home let to the company, and what Netto cannot see of a company car: whether the company pays the private fuel or charging, what drives the car, its CO2 as registered or that none is, the date it was ordered or leased, whether a plug-in hybrid is a fake one with its matching car's CO2, and whether a natural-gas car is under 12 fiscal horsepower; and of a copyright royalty: whether the director's copyright income of the four previous years averaged within the cap, whether they hold an attestation du travail des arts covering its works when it is paid, never the starter attestation, and the share of their working time spent creating protected works, which the royalty reads when it follows the pay) and shows its conditions. A car with a combustion engine whose CO2 is not given is refused, never taken at an example (operator, 2026-10-22). When the company pays the contributions on the director's behalf they are taxable pay, deducted as the director's own: the split's pay stays the gross on the payslip, the company's cost (gross plus contributions) comes off its profit, and the art. 215 minimum pay is tested on the taxable pay. A condition on the scenario's own figures is the engine's to test, because the levers move those figures: the minimum director pay that keeps the reduced corporate rate, tested on the taxable pay with the benefits in kind counted, and those benefits, after the director's contribution, within 20% of it (strictly; art. 215 al. 3, 5°, in the first four tax periods too).
- **User data stays in the browser**: browser storage and file export, no accounts, nothing sent to a server. The Plans panel exports every plan with the star as one file in the stored format and imports a file's readable plans after the ones here, never replacing one, each under a new id, its « Today » starred only while none is (`SavedPlans.Export`, `Import`; the download in `Netto.Web/wwwroot/plan-file.js`): the browser writes and reads the file, so a file is read exactly as strictly as storage. `SavedPlans.Changed` tells a listener each time the plans are kept (saved, renamed, starred, deleted, opened, imported), after they are written; a component that listens disposes its subscription. Saved plans outlive the code, so they are stored in a versioned format of their own (`PlanStorage`, never `RiverInputs` serialised as it is) and read strictly: a plan is read as it was saved or marked unreadable, never filled with defaults, and a document that cannot be read is copied aside before anything is written over it. An amount the example holds none of (`InputOrigin.None`) is stored as « Example », as the format always held it, and a stored « Example » zero of money or a count is read back as none, in a plan of any version and in a file imported, so the format did not change for it (decided unattended, 2026-10-23). A new input of the river is a new field of the format, a new `PlanStorage.Version` (`RiverInputsShapeTests` fails until it is), one patch from the version before, and that version's captured document in the tests' `StoredPlanDocuments` with the inputs it means typed out. `PlanStorage.Version` is 6 since what the same car would cost privately; a plan of an older version is brought to it one version at a time, on a copy, by a patch per version (`PlanStorage.Patches`) that adds what that version never held as frozen literal JSON, never from `RiverInputs.Example`, and refuses a plan already holding it (the copy's parse refuses a member held twice), then read with the one strict `StoredInputs`: each plan as saved, without a car, a royalty, a bill or a private cost it did not hold, a royalty the user typed in format 3 or 4 opening fixed at it and any other following the pay, written back as 6, and a plan it cannot read kept exactly as stored. A story may add inputs to the format it raised without raising it again while that format has not left its story branch: the car's engine, CO2, order date and statuses joined format 2, the royalty's attestation and creative share joined format 3, so a plan saved by such a story's first build does not open (operator, 2026-10-22; decided unattended for the royalty); any later new input raises the version.
- **The payment calendar is a record beside the ledger, built by the engine** (`Netto.Core/Calendar/PaymentCalendar`, decided unattended 2026-10-22), as the pay statement is: a step is the year's total and carries no date; the calendar places the year's own figures in time, seen on the browser's date. It holds the company's and the director's advance-payment schedules by the income year's deadlines (`RuleDate`), each past or not, and, only at the first deadline still open (`PaymentCalendar.FirstOpen`, open until the end of its day), what more cancels the surcharge: each advance-payment result gives that for every deadline (`TopUps`), and the moves' payment finders read the calendar's figure, so what the moves propose and what the calendar says are one number. It also holds the statement's months, INASTI's quarters (a quarter of the final contribution, which `SocialContributionCalculator.Billed` makes exact) and the balance on each assessment of the next tax year, the company's being its tax with the surcharge less what it paid in advance (art. 304, §2, al. 2). Its one input, what the fund bills each quarter (`RiverInputs.BilledContributions`, none until given, never an example), changes no figure of the river: `PaymentCalendar.WithBilled` adds the regularisation, the river's figures keeping the final contribution.
- **The site is one page at `/plan`, in tabs by purpose** (« Goal & scope », « How the site is organised »): Plan, Optimisation, When to pay and Compare plans, under the bar of buckets, which every tab shows. A rule amount is formatted by its key's suffix (`Formats.RuleAmount`: `_rate` or `_factor` a percent, `_multiplier`, `_coefficient`, `_grams` an emission in g/km, anything else euros), so the key of a rate or a share ends in `_rate`. A car's CO2 percentage or deduction rate keeps its decimals (`Formats.Percentage`: 9.75%, 61.6%). A rate a card states in a line or a bubble comes from its rule set's `RuleAmount` through `Formats.RuleAmount`, never typed into the text and never through `WholeShare`, which drops decimals (the car's and the royalty's rates, 2026-10-23).
- **A numeric field the engine validates carries no `Min`/`Max`**: MudBlazor would clamp the value silently and the engine's error key, with its message, would never show.
- **Nothing is prerendered, so the page is empty while the runtime downloads.** `App.razor` shows a splash, and `app.css` hides it as soon as `.netto-app` (the layout's root) exists. A layout without that class leaves the splash on screen.
- Blazor gotcha: **a link to `/#section` from another page lands at the top**. Blazor scrolls to a fragment only on a same-page link; after a cross-page navigation, `blazor.web.js` resets the scroll to the top once the new page has rendered, undoing any scroll towards the section. So the header's section links render only on the front page, and the front page scrolls to one of its own sections itself when opened directly (`/#principles`), for a fixed list of ids, because the fragment becomes a CSS selector.
- Blazor gotcha: **two routes of one component keep it alive.** Between `/plan` and `/plan/{Tab}` the page is not rebuilt and `FocusOnNavigate` does not move the focus (it fires only when the page type changes); `blazor.web.js` resets the scroll to the top after a link to another path, at the end of the render batch and before `OnAfterRenderAsync` (smoothly here, since `app.css` sets `scroll-behavior`), but never on Back or Forward. So `TabHead` focuses its own h1, and what must scroll after arriving waits for the next frame (`nettoOutline.arrive`).

## Testing

- **Test-first in `Netto.Core`.** `Netto.Tests.Unit` mirrors Core's folders (the benefits in kind and the movable income tested under `DirectorPay/`, where the pay takes them), plus `Architecture/` for the guards on the project graph, and runs without Docker.
- **Shared test support lives in `tests/Netto.Tests.Unit/Support/`**, in scope everywhere through a global using of `Netto.Tests.Support`: the in-memory `BrowserStorage` and the `RefusingStorage` fakes, `Clock` (a fixed `TimeProvider`), `RepositoryFile.FindUp` (a checkout file from a test), `ResxText` (the one reader of a component's `.resx`: `For<T>()`, `Get`, `Format`, `Missing`, `Keys`; `InterfaceTexts` reads through it) and `Fixtures` (`using static`: `Scenario(...)` with the made-up company's defaults, `Run`, `Search`, `RiverExample`, the lever fixtures, `UnsupportedIncomeYear`). A test never redeclares one of them: `Architecture/TestSupportTests` scans the test sources and fails on a private fake, a `ResourceManager`, a `Scenario` factory or a literal unsupported year. A new lever is one new argument of `Fixtures.Scenario`.
- **A card's or a page's own logic is a view model, tested first too**: plain C# in `Netto.Web.Client` (`internal`, visible to the tests) that turns the engine's result into what it draws (`RiverInputs`, `BucketsView`, `River/ModuleViews.cs`), tested under `Netto.Tests.Unit/WebClient/`. The `.razor` file only binds it. The view model never decides a tax outcome; it selects, orders and scales the engine's steps.
- **A component's first draw is tested without a browser**: `WebClient/River/RiverRendering` renders a card, a `Fold`, a tab or the `Plan` page on any tab (its `Tab` route parameter) to HTML with the framework's `HtmlRenderer` (no package), as a viewer meets it on first load (`FoldTests`, `CardFoldTests`, `PlanPageTests`). It runs no click and no JavaScript (`IJSRuntime` and `NavigationManager` are fakes that throw), so what a click does is a view model's test or the browser check. The Plan page's first draw therefore shows the moves being looked for; `WebClient/Pages/LivePlan` loads the page as the browser runs it, its work after each render included, with a script runtime that records each call and may hold the paint the page waits for (`PlanArrivalTests`, `PlanMovesSearchTests`), and what it drew is read from the parameters its components were handed, since it writes no HTML. `WebClient/River/CardHtml` reads a rendered card as a viewer reads it: its summary and rate, its prose, its lines and their amounts, its visible words (the bubble texts and hidden elements left out, a word holding a letter or a digit: the count the browser's measure matches) and any resx key leaked onto the page, and renders the Plan page with every card open (`RenderPlanOpenAsync`, the clock fixed); a card's word budget and the shape of its summary are tested through it, and `CardHtml.BubbleAfter` reads the bubble named « More about » a label. `WebClient/InterfaceTexts` enumerates the `.resx` texts and counts a sentence's words by the same rule; `RiverRendering` renders a card by its `Type` too, open or with its details open; a static render opens a `Disclosure` through a test-only `IComponentActivator` (`StreamCardsFiguresTests.OpenDisclosures`), never a production seam.
- **The year charges both advance-payment surcharges unless the scenario prepays**: the company's on its corporate tax, and the director's on the income tax on the pay when too little of it was withheld or paid in advance. The river's example prepays nothing by the company and withholds on the pay as the key formula says (`TaxPrepayments.AsWithheld`, decided 2026-10-20), so it shows the company's surcharge and the balance left on the assessment. A test about something else prepays, so its figures stay those of the layer it is about: `YearSimulationTests` and `SplitSearchTests` default to a `Prepaid` amount by 10 April, `GrossToNetTests`, `YearSimulationTests` and `SplitSearchTests` to a `Withheld` amount beyond any tax, and the web tests to a prepaid copy of the example that withholds a given amount.
- **A decimal can be a negative zero** (`0m - 0.00m`). `ShouldBe` and record equality take it for 0, but `ArgumentOutOfRangeException.ThrowIfNegative` rejects it and a drain reads it as money coming back (`decimal.IsNegative`), so only the browser showed the crash: guard with `ThrowIfLessThan(x, 0m)`, return a plain `0m` for what moves nothing, and test a zero line with `decimal.IsNegative`. Engine code subtracts through `Money.Minus` and takes cent bounds through `Money.DownToCent` and `UpToCent`, which never return a negative zero (`MoneyTests`); the web client keeps its own idioms for now.
- **No real figures in the repository.** Tests use made-up cases. The operator's own figures live under `/local/`, which git ignores; never copy them into a test, a commit message or Notion.
- A personal-tax expected value comes from a Tax-Calc run on the same inputs, and the test names the income year it covers. For a year Tax-Calc does not cover yet, it is derived from the official amounts and the test says so.
- **The monthly withholding has no Tax-Calc check**: Tax-Calc takes it as an input (code 1407). Its expected values are derived from the key formula and cross-checked against the formula chain of the SPF simulator's « Dirigeants d'entreprise » sheet, read out of the xlsx (no Excel on this machine runs it), and the test says so. The balance on the assessment is held to Tax-Calc's « SOLDE » line, the one before the special social security contribution (T12, 2026-10-20).
- **Tax-Calc** (the SPF's anonymous simulator, latest income year only) is driven in the browser, and each submission needs the operator's go. « Encodage rapide » takes codes 1001 (single: a box in cadre II, which its validation links to), 1400 (director pay), 1405 (own contributions), 1407 (withholding: required whenever 1400 is filled, 0 is fine) 1437 (withholding on the dividends exempt up to 833, listed as a refundable element) 1571 to 1574 (the director's advance payments, one per quarter), 1109 and 1110 (the non-indexed cadastral income and the gross rent of a building let to a company for its professional use, cadre III, A, 5, a), and 1123, 1124 and 1119 (a copyright royalty's gross part within its limits, its costs as typed, since Tax-Calc never applies the flat rate itself, and its précompte mobilier, cadre VII, D); cadre I needs a postal code, the municipal rate and a birth date. A director's benefit in kind has no code of its own: it goes into 1400 with the pay, as the 281.20 sums them (T15, 2026-10-22); so does a royalty's part beyond its limits (T18). Entering a code again adds a second line instead of replacing the first: start a new form (« Accéder à Tax-Calc ») for each run. Read « Détail du calcul »: the tax credit for low earned income is the line « Faibles revenus d'activités » under « Crédits d'impôt », and « Majoration pour absence ou insuffisance de versement anticipé » and « Bonification » detail the advance-payment surcharge and bonus line by line, the surcharge on the tax recomputed on the director pay alone (« Revenus soumis à majoration »). Its question on the special social security contribution defaults to « Oui »: answer « Non », since a self-employed person in main occupation does not owe it (`official-docs/social-contributions/`). The 2026-10-19 runs found no field for it in the anonymous form; its line comes after the ones Netto compares, so read those.

## Definition of done

A slice (or any task) is done only when ALL of:

1. `dotnet build` clean and `dotnet test` green, run from the repo root. The build enforces it: `TreatWarningsAsErrors` with the analysers pinned at `10.0-recommended` in `Directory.Build.props` (the NuGet audit's NU1900 to NU1904 stay warnings, CA1716 is off repo-wide, CA1707 off in tests for the `Method_scenario_expected` names; `BuildPolicyTests` holds the settings).
2. The acceptance criteria of the slice's Notion item are met and demonstrated in the running app (browser-verified by the hivemind, through the AppHost or `Netto.Web` alone on 5290: « Parallel work »), not only in tests.
3. Docs are true again: « Goal & scope » edited where the contract changed, any convention here that the slice changed updated, the River Map updated where the river changed, its diagrams with it, `official-docs/` holding the current edition of every document the slice's rule sets cite, and the Notion item moved to `Done`.
4. The story branch's commits are listed slice by slice (contents + messages), with the decision log, and the fast-forward onto `main` is operator-approved. Pushing, once a remote exists, needs separate explicit approval.
