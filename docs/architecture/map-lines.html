How My plan's map draws its lines

My plan, read by bucket, is a map of the plan's modules and the money between them, drawn by whose money each module moves, with the chosen module's card beside it. The map computes no figure: every line of money is a line a card already shows, every condition line is one the engine names, and the one sum of its own, the pocket line's running total, adds those lines. This page follows the page's one run to the drawn map.

As of feature/three-views 232a94111 November 2026

Three columns, two lines

The map has three columns by owner (MapOwner): you and your household (Household above Director pay), ways across (each lever in the plan, the royalty and the dividend, drawn as dashed gates), and the company (Company year, To share, Corporate tax). Rows keep the river's order. Two lines run down it:

  • The company's line, down the left of the company's column from Company year into the node « To share ». Each lever takes its share across to its gate at a point beside it (MapEnd.Beside), the share its card's « The company pays »; the line's amount after each point is the card's « Left after … ». The car takes no share: its costs leave the line into Expenses and the viewer's contribution joins it. Points where nothing leaves or joins are folded past (MapQuiet.Fold).
  • The pocket line, gold, down the left of your column into Your pocket. Every line into the pocket joins it at its module's point (MapEnd.OnPocketLine); after each join it says what has reached you so far, its last total the bar's pocket. The car's contribution leaves it at the car's row (MapPocketLine).

A gate says what crosses and what reaches you (MapGates, MapGateWords); the pay crosses under a « Pay · €x » pill, two lines (« Pay + contributions » over its amount) when the company pays the contributions; a badge fits its box at the narrowest map, a tax base's showing its amount and leaving its words heard; Household's box says its quotient. An amount is said once (MapLink.Unsaid): a join, the pay, a tax base into Corporate tax, and a line into a gate whose box says the same amount carry none. Decision: the map drawn by owner.

From the run to the drawing

Tabs/MapTab.razor receives the page's inputs and its one RiverRun and rebuilds its view only when either changes. MapView.From gathers the modules in the plan, their links and the conditions, gathers what reaches you into the pocket line, then MapLayout places and routes them; the module chosen decides which tax-base and condition lines are drawn, which moves nothing.

The four kinds of line

KindMeansDrawn
PassedOnMoney one module hands the next, or the company's lineSolid, width by amount, arrowhead, always
IntoBucketMoney that ends in a bucket, the pocket line among themSolid, width by amount, ends at the bucket's edge; gold into the pocket; always
TaxBaseMoney that only changes a tax base: a benefit taxed with the pay, what Corporate tax adds back, the partner's quotientDotted, fixed width, for the module chosen
ConditionA condition one module sets on anotherDashed, fixed width, for the module chosen

Lines of money run from 1.5 to 8 px wide, by the square root of their share of the run's largest line (MapLineWidths).

Modules, in MapModules.Order: Company year, Vouchers, Allowances, Home office rent, Company car, To share (RiverModule.Split, named for the money it holds), Copyright royalty, Director pay, Household, Corporate tax, Dividend. Buckets, in BucketsView.Keys: your pocket, taxes on pay, taxes on profit, kept in company, expenses. A new module is its place in MapModules.Order and MapLayout.Place (its owner's column), its lines in MapLinks.Of, a gate's lines in MapGates, its point on the pocket line if it reaches you, its text and its MapPanel arm.
