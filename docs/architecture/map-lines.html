How the Plan tab's map draws its lines

The Plan tab is a map of the plan's modules and the money between them, with the chosen module's card beside it. The map computes no figure: every line of money is a line a card already shows, and every condition line is one the engine names. This page follows the page's one run to the drawn map.

As of main 29cbc9529 October 2026

From the run to the drawing

Tabs/MapTab.razor receives the page's inputs and its one RiverRun and rebuilds its view only when either changes. MapView.From gathers the modules in the plan, their links and the conditions, then MapLayout places and routes them; the viewer's legend filter hides kinds of line, which moves nothing.

The four kinds of line

KindMeansDrawn
PassedOnMoney one module hands the nextSolid, width by amount, arrowhead
IntoBucketMoney that ends in a bucketSolid, width by amount, ends at the bucket's edge
TaxBaseMoney that only changes a tax base: a benefit taxed with the pay, the partner's quotientDotted, fixed width
ConditionA condition one module sets on anotherDashed, fixed width, hidden by default

Lines of money run from 1.5 to 8 px wide, by the square root of their share of the run's largest line (MapLineWidths).

Modules, in MapModules.Order: Company year, Vouchers, Allowances, Home office rent, Company car, The split, Copyright royalty, Director pay, Household, Corporate tax, Dividend. Buckets, in BucketsView.Keys: your pocket, taxes on pay, taxes on profit, kept in company, expenses. A new module is its place in MapModules.Order and MapLayout.Place, its lines in MapLinks.Of, its text and its MapPanel arm.
