What happens when you edit a field

An amount typed on a card travels up to the plan page, which builds new inputs, runs the year once in the engine and hands that one result to the bar, the map and the card. This page follows the revenue field of the Company year card from keystroke to repaint, and says what an edit does not do.

As of feature/french-by-default b1d21ed30 October 2026

One edit, step by step

Inputs are immutable records: a card never changes them, it raises InputsChanged with a copy (Inputs.WithRevenue(…)), marked as the user's own. Pages/Plan.razor is the one owner of the plan's inputs and of the run made from them; everything the user sees is drawn from that run on the next render.

What an edit does not do

  • It saves nothing. RiverState holds the inputs in memory; only the Plans panel writes them to storage, and a switch of language carries them across its reload in sessionStorage (netto.river). The panel's « unsaved changes » dot reacts at once (SavedPlans.HasUnsavedChanges). See how a plan is saved.
  • It does not search the moves. The Optimisation tab's moves are measured from a baseline that an edit does not change, so MovesSource has nothing to search. See how the moves are found and the decision.
  • It computes no tax in the page. Every figure on the bar, map and card comes from the engine's run: one year simulation, map lines read from the cards.

One edit runs the engine more than once: PayCeiling runs its own walk before Run, the split's search runs the year at many pays, and the map's conditions (YearConditions.Of) search the smallest pay that keeps the reduced corporate rate.
