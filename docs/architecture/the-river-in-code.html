How one income year runs through the engine

The river a user sees, money flowing from the company's year through the modules to their pocket, is one call in code: YearSimulation.Run(Scenario). It walks the year in the order the law computes it and returns one YearResult that every card, the bar and the map read. This page shows that walk, what each lever hands the next layer, and where to add a lever or an income year.

The walk through one year

The page turns its inputs into a Scenario, gated by what is in the plan. Run takes INASTI's yearly company contribution off what the company earns, then folds over the levers in LeverOrder.For(year): vouchers, expense allowances, home office rent, company car, copyright royalty. Each lever takes its amount off what is left and says what reaches the pocket, what the company cannot deduct and what it hands the pay to be taxed with it. What the levers leave is the profit before pay the split shares, the node « To share » on the map. GrossToNet taxes the pay with what the levers handed it, corporate tax takes the rest, and the dividend pays out what is left above the reserve. Every layer reads its rules for the income year and region; nothing in the walk tests the year itself.

What each lever hands the year

A lever's LeverYear says four things: what reaches the pocket besides the pay (ToPocket), what the company cannot deduct or adds back (Disallowed), what it hands the pay to be taxed with it (TaxedWithPay), and its steps, before the company's and, for a tax of its own, after the pay's (StepsAfterPay).

Adding to it

To addTouch
A leverIts facts on Scenario; a Lever and LeverYear in Simulation/Levers/; its place in LeverOrder.For(year); a YearResult field if its card reads one; an argument of Fixtures.Scenario. On the site, its module, card and map arms (CLAUDE.md lists them).
An income yearA new instance of each rule set (IncomeYear2027), its case in each For, every amount sourced to a document official-docs/INDEX.md lists as current for that year. Never an edit of the old instance, never a test on the year.
A refusalA static readonly Refusal beside its rule, its RefusedInput and owner in RiverErrors.Owner, its words as Error_<key> in RefusedMessage.resx: a typed value.
A condition between modulesA YearCondition record, its case in YearConditions.Of, its arm in Tabs/MapConditions: the engine names them.
