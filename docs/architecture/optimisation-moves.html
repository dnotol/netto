How Optimise finds its moves and Ideas its ideas

The moves are what Netto would retune in the plan on screen to put more in the director's pocket, using only the levers the plan already holds and the facts it was given. An idea is a lever the plan does not use, its gain the optimised plan with it less the optimised plan without it. Both are searched after the page has painted, found greedily by the engine, and changed in the plan only by « Apply » and « Keep ». This page follows one search of the moves, one search of an idea, and the pay curve that sets the plan's own pay.

One search, then Apply

The moves are measured from the plan on screen, the river the band runs: no baseline is kept, and « Today », the starred plan, feeds only the band's gain. MovesSource, built once by the plan page, keeps the last result for one plan and one date, so a search runs again as soon as an edit, an opened plan or the day makes that result stale. Until then Optimise and the badges say they are looking for the moves, and no stale move is ever listed or applied.

The ideas

An idea is a lever the plan does not hold whose gain in cash the engine measures from the facts the viewer gives: the vouchers, the home office rent and the copyright royalty (PisteSearch.Levers). A move that switched such a lever on would rest on conditions Netto cannot see, so it is not one of Optimise's moves: the idea first asks what its card holds and Netto cannot see (PisteTrial.Questions), and shows no gain before they are answered. Its gain is plan against plan: the pocket of the optimised plan with the lever, less the pocket of the optimised plan without it, both runs of the year. The « without » side is the plan the moves search above already found, shared by every idea.

The pay curve

Under the moves, Optimise draws the curve of the pocket at every pay (PayCurve): the question of tuning the pay, drawn. It reads the page's own search of the split (RiverInputs.SearchSplit, run again on an edit unless only the pay moved), with the plan's own pay marked and the best pay marked. A click on the curve, or « move to the best pay », sets the plan's own pay, said as such: it is an edit like any other and no move of the list, which are applied only by « Apply ». The pay itself is typed on the Director pay card; the node « To share » on the map holds a read-only card. The moves are stacked, each found on top of those above it, while the curve and the When to pay section are for the plan on screen: their headings say so, and a note says why a move's pay or payment can differ from theirs. A move whose sentence states a figure that is not its gain (vouchers, a rent, the company's surcharge) bridges the two in that sentence (MoveReasons.Bridged).
