How Optimise finds its moves

The moves are what Netto would retune in the plan on screen to put more in the director's pocket, using only the levers the plan already holds and the facts it was given. A lever the plan does not hold is no move: it is found and added in « Edit plan », over the map. The moves are searched after the page has painted, found greedily by the engine, and changed in the plan only by « Apply » and « Apply all ». This page follows one search of the moves, and the three sub-tabs of Optimise that show them, the pay curve and the payment calendar.

One search, then Apply

The moves are measured from the plan on screen, the river the band runs: no baseline is kept, and « Today », the starred plan, feeds only the band's gain. MovesSource, built once by the plan page, keeps the last result for one plan and one date, so a search runs again as soon as an edit, an opened plan or the day makes that result stale. Until then Optimise and the badges say they are looking for the moves, and no stale move is ever listed or applied.

The three sub-tabs

Optimise is one head line and three sub-tabs, links in a nav, only the chosen one drawn (OptimisationTab, OptimiseSection). The head line, common to the three, says what the plan leaves in the pocket, what it leaves with every move and the difference, with « Apply all » at its end. The address holds the choice (OptimiseSections.From reads the show query, OptimiseSections.Href writes it), so a sub-tab is a bookmark and Back and Forward walk them: the moves at /plan/optimisation, the pay curve at ?show=pay, When to pay at ?show=when. OptimiseArrival tells an arrival at a sub-tab, other than by a click on the row, so the page brings its panel into focus.

The pay curve

The pay sub-tab draws the curve of the pocket at every pay (PayCurve): the question of tuning the pay, drawn. It reads the page's own search of the split (RiverInputs.SearchSplit, run again on an edit unless only the pay moved), with the plan's own pay marked and the best pay marked. A click on the curve, or « move to the best pay », sets the plan's own pay, said as such: it is an edit like any other and no move of the list, which are applied only by « Apply ». The pay itself is typed on the Director pay card; the node « To share » on the map holds a read-only card. The moves are stacked, each found on top of those above it, while the curve and the When to pay section are for the plan on screen: their headings say so, and a note says why a move's pay or payment can differ from theirs. A move whose sentence states a figure that is not its gain (vouchers, a rent, the company's surcharge) bridges the two in that sentence (MoveReasons.Bridged).
