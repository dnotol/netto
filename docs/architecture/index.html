Netto · architecture

How Netto is built

Netto tells a Belgian SRL owner-director how to pay themselves so the most money reaches their pocket. It is one Blazor WebAssembly app: the tax engine runs in the browser, and the user's plans never leave it. Each page below answers one question, drawn from the code; the decisions behind them are in the decision record.

Decisions

Every decision taken is an ADR in decisions/, dated the day it was decided, grouped by what it shapes; decisions/log.md records what changed. A decision still under debate lives on its story in Notion, « Netto Development », and lands here once taken.
