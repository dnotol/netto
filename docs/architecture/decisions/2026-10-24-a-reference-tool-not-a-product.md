# Context

Until 24.10.2026 Netto was planned as a product: competitors, monetisation, a freemium cut, a name and trademark and demand validation were researched (Notion, « Commercial research », [shelved 2026-10-24](https://app.notion.com/p/403b6b39bd1281bb8608d7f168d9828f)). An external review found no realistic revenue.

# Decision

The operator dropped the commercial goal. Netto is a reference tool for its operator and a few people they trust, and runs locally: no deadline, no freemium, no public repository required, private hosting when the first trusted person needs it. The product contract is the Notion page « [Goal & scope](https://app.notion.com/p/3fdb6b39bd1281bc81bfce270c576635) ».

# Alternatives rejected

- A commercial product with a freemium split, as researched before 24.10.2026: no realistic revenue found by the external review.

# Consequences

- In scope next: the pensions (EIP/IPT, VAPZ/PLCI), the liquidation reserve and pillar 3, the household (partner and children, built on 28 and 29.10.2026) and other income on the return, a side-activity status, a two-year horizon.
- Dropped: Flanders and Brussels (the region seam stays, [region is a parameter](/2026-10-24-region-is-a-parameter-of-every-rule-lookup.md)), a phone pass, per-slice WCAG checks; the word budgets and their tests went too.
- The home office rent is frozen as built.
- `CLAUDE.md` is held to at most 4,000 words.
